# Wedding music

`wedding-music.mp3` is a copy of the existing previous wedding invitation's
configured `dheeni-premantara.mp3` track. The original remains unchanged.

Playback starts only when the guest presses Play music. The local audio loops,
loads on demand, and stops when the invitation unmounts for replay. Volume is adjusted with the device volume buttons; the on-page volume slider was removed. No third-party music player or tracking scripts are used.
