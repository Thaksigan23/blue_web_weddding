# Wedding music

`wedding-music.mp3` is a copy of the existing previous wedding invitation's
configured `dheeni-premantara.mp3` track. The original remains unchanged.

Playback starts only when the guest presses Play music. The local audio loops,
loads on demand, and stops when the invitation unmounts for replay. The volume
panel offers a slider; iOS versions that enforce system volume require the
device volume buttons. No third-party music player or tracking scripts are used.
